# Mixed licensing: separate expressions, files and choices

This is a **review protocol, not license terms**. Record the precise SPDX expression and affected component files and versions. `A AND B` requires satisfying both terms; `A OR B` requires a real, documented choice supported by the source's original grant, not merely a convenient scanner label. Parenthesize nested expressions, and retain the verbatim upstream notice and chosen grant.

An approved BSD/MIT identifier does not wash away a forbidden GNU obligation under `AND`. **All LGPL versions, including LGPL-2.1-only and LGPL-2.1-or-later, are FORBIDDEN under the current no-GNU rule.** Those two LGPL reference texts may be byte-identical even though the underlying component's grants differ, so both remain in the reference cache for evidence, not admission. An `OR` license alternative may be considered only after the actual source grant supports a valid non-GNU option and the operator explicitly approves it. Unresolved scope or conflicting interpretations mean **HOLD**.

Add the component's provenance, notice file path, hashes, chosen alternative, obligations, and explicit reviewer approval to [COMPONENT_USAGE.tsv](COMPONENT_USAGE.tsv); retain historic interpretations in Git history.
