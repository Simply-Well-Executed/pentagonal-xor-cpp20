# DIHAODA Wizmacker

Copr.© 2026 by PAUL ALLEN CHEEK mail@pcheek.com C5824

DIHAODA is an independently explorable topology lab, distinct from DIAERY. All gates must be visible and keyboard, touch and assistive pointer accessible. `[XOR][AIRGAP][OR]` is the default, not a limit. Custom COR and COREAO behaviors require explicit definitions; no implicit route choice. Keep free access and preserve data and provenance. Release HOLD until native tests pass.


## Current authorized Wizmacker palette

`[AND][OR][NOT][NAND][NOR][XOR][XNOR][COR][COREAO][NOP][NIL][PASS][AIRGAP][QUARAN]`

`[BUF]` and `[QUAREN]` are **excluded** from the current DIHAODA gate vocabulary by explicit user direction. `[QUARAN]` is the requested spelling and is a selectable **symbolic operator**, not an assumed synonym for QUAREN and not an automatically executable gate. Its semantics require explicit definition and validation. The assistant-introduced `[MUX]`, `[IMPLIES]` and `[NIMPLIES]` and the category `Additional` are also excluded. See [PROVENANCE_CORRECTION.md](PROVENANCE_CORRECTION.md). This applies to the DIHAODA Wizmacker palette, not every unrelated occurrence of words such as buffer or boolean implication in other projects.
