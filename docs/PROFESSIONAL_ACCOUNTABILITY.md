# Professional accountability: ANYthang scope creep and LODESTAR

Status: project-local proposed engineering controls, 2026-11-27. RELEASE=HOLD. Not accounting, audit, legal or regulatory certification.

## Origin and purpose

The operator identified ANYthang as a risk of adopting technology before competence, ethical safeguards, staffing and accountability are established. Capability is not authority. A title, automated result, or role rotation does not confer a credential or permission. Define work by responsibility, competence, jurisdiction, and explicit delegation rather than title alone.

## Five ethical principles translated into project controls

| Principle | Proposed repository control |
| --- | --- |
| Integrity | Retain original records, failures and source provenance; no invented pass |
| Objectivity | Separate creator and reviewer; disclose conflicts; independently check critical outcomes |
| Professional competence and due care | Document scope, skills, limitations and tests; escalate work outside competence |
| Confidentiality | Minimize data, restrict access, exclude private records from public PRs |
| Professional behavior | Preserve rights and approvals; avoid misleading credentials or certification claims |

These are engineering analogies, not a claim of IESBA compliance.

## Technology adoption and separation of duties

Before use: name purpose, owner, affected people, data and rights, competence, known risks, alternatives, validation plan, rollback and approval scope. Unknown or missing material evidence means HOLD for the affected operation. Use proportionate controls; do not impose a professional signoff requirement on ordinary low-risk mathematical experimentation.

For material changes, separate preparation, independent review and authorized approval. Automation may collect evidence and flag discrepancies but cannot invent reviewer independence, credentials or consent. If separation is infeasible, disclose conflict and obtain a suitable independent compensating review before material approval. Record actual role, authority, source revision, evidence, decision, dissent and limitations. Role rotation never inherits approval automatically.

## LODESTAR connection

LODESTAR preserves canonical attribution and source identity. PENNYWORTH attribution is distinct from mathematical qualification. PENCE investigates unresolved provenance or rights overlap; similarity is not proof of ownership. FREERADICAL verifies its own scoped mathematics, not financial statements. DOVER preserves failed evidence and recovery receipts. Neither an AI assistant nor a successful test becomes a CPA, auditor, attorney or executive by performing a related task.

Acceptance witnesses (proposed, not executed): missing reviewer, role collision, invalid credential claim, stale source revision, unauthorized disclosure, missing rights evidence, untested technology and attempted self-approval each require explicit handling or HOLD. A complete record permits consideration by the authorized reviewer, not automatic approval. No new runtime dependencies, network calls, credentials or release authorization.
