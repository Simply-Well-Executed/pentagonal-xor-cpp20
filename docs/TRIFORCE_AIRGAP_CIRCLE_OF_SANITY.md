## AIRGAP centrix: Circle of Sanity (not a fourth triangle)

The user's correction is canonical for this v1 pipeline design: the three
separate Euler, ℼ and I **triangles** are joined by one **circular**, read-only
AIRGAP centrix. Its loop *PING* is a local validation event, not an ICMP
request, a network transmission or a fourth numerical input.

Each triangle has a preserved owner source record, a Q62 arithmetic witness,
and a rightmost-two-digit (`23`) check. The center inspects all three,
confirms they remain **distinct**, and yields only a status:
`all_consistent` or **HOLD**. Source mutation is never the response to a
failed check. The center has `release_authorized=false` unconditionally.

Diagram: `(Euler △) → (AIRGAP ○) ← (ℼ △)`; `(I △) → (AIRGAP ○)`.
The center is a **circle**, not a fourth triangle, and neither shared
metadata nor a common suffix merges the three mathematical identities.

A valid status proves consistency against the declared frozen records,
not the correctness of an independent physical theory. A malicious actor
who can rewrite both records and all checking policy can still undermine
the assurance, so independent protected review remains necessary.


Sources of authority are still the three user comments and
`shared/toolong_q62_core.hpp`. `ci/Assert-TriforceHeadTail.ps1` checks
the immutable source lines with .NET BigInteger, all three quotient results
and their 23 suffix, and the circle's fail-closed contract, before and after
each controlled operation. `tests/triforce_native_gate_test.cpp`
independently executes the TOOLONG audit and the circle invariants under
native MSVC C++20 /O2.

The Circle is metadata and integrity routing, not a person, owner, vote,
network access credential, authority, authenticated signature, or proof
that light obeys a proposed numerical SigmaPi substitution.

The AIRGAP is a **logical verification boundary**: the Q62 loop performs no network PING. GitHub-hosted pipelines still require an explicitly disclosed network source-fetch bootstrap; this is not a claim that the entire CI runner is physically air-gapped. Source-byte SHA-256 inventory covers all files inside the fetched source snapshot, not just the constants.
