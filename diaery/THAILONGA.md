# THAILONGA | Unified DYNTR0X and PROJECTIONS

**EXPLORATORY | PR #316 | 0BSD | RELEASE=HOLD**

THAILONGA binds two previously separate presentation aspects to **one immutable input selection and one derived evaluation**:

1. **DYNTR0X subject / physical-unit graph:** positive penile length X and circumference Y in inches; independent vaginal depth Z; FILTREX excludes zero/missing/negative and nonfinite values; FILTROX checks source-specific observed ranges. The selected point S and the algebraic intercept line are different objects, not observed regression.
2. **PROJECTIONS transparencies:** ten bounded `{0}`..`{9}` choices; 0=source, 1=intercept, 2=identity, 3=baseline render when eligible; 4=mean, 5=threshold, 6=rolling mean, 7=regression, 8=confidence interval, 9=circle remain NULL pending proper evidence. The exploratory historical 41-42% female orgasm band is in a distinct normalized bar with `SOURCE_UNVERIFIED / NULL` and has no implied causal relationship to anatomy.

The two aspects share input data, eligibility, source-range status, projection availability, uncertainty NULL, and the same exact femtosecond ledger. No aspect mutates the other or overwrites its source. A selected study-bound comparison is not an actual study participant. Similarity of representations is not identity of data or evidence.

**Native integration:** `thailonga.hpp` computes a single compile-time-compatible `Frame`; `diaery.cpp` reads that shared frame in native DYNTR0X painting; `thailonga_test.cpp` tests eligible values, zero/missing rejection, small positive but outside sample observations, NULL scientific overlays, and preserved source/time. CTest and the per-rung LadderDi native list both contain `thailonga_test.cpp`.

**Controls:** arrows for X/Y; PageUp/PageDown Z; K study/exploratory gears; G quarter-X/third-Y grid; 0..9 toggle projection; P select projection; R enter exact `{n}`; N toggle the independent unverified band; F +1fs; S +1s. No general expression engine is exposed.

**Unresolved scientific prerequisites:** original Copilot+ JSONL for band outcome and denominator; real paired anatomy measurements; statistical interval methodology; clinical study comparability and uncertainty. Never infer orgasm outcomes from geometric extrema or interpolate them into a real measured probability. Do not promote release without native execution and source verification.
